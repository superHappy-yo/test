\begin{abstract}
Whether a tensor operator is compute-bound or memory-bound depends on shape, dtype, and which intermediates touch HBM---not on the operator name alone.
On an H100-class Roofline, a width-4096 FP16 GEMM crosses from arithmetic intensity (AI) $\approx 1$ at decode to AI $\approx 1024$ at prefill; IO-aware attention crosses with sequence length while a score-materializing plan does not; a skewed mixture-of-experts (MoE) layer can hold both regimes in one forward pass.
Fusion frozen at operator kind cannot follow those crossings.

We present a \emph{shape-conditioned intensity classifier} (SCIC) that labels each producer--consumer site before scheduling, and a \emph{dual-plan} policy that emits MemMode (memory-oriented fusion), ComputeMode (compute-oriented fission), or both in a band around the datasheet ridge.
Relative to joint MatMul--epilogue fusion, the contribution is classification and runtime plan selection---not custom fused CUDA kernels, Stage-Stream execution, or a new attention algorithm.

The classifier, plan table, and figures in our artifact are analytical.
Device evidence follows a deliberate pipeline: reproduce closed forms; time stock \texttt{torch.matmul} on an NVIDIA A40 as shape-dependence motivation only; then run Docker wall-clock bakeoffs comparing eager, \texttt{torch.compile}, library \texttt{scaled\_dot\_product\_attention}, and SCIC-selected paths.
Nsight DRAM bytes failed with \texttt{ERR\_NVGPUCTRPERM}, so achieved AI, a measured ridge, and end-to-end tokens/s remain future work; \texttt{quote\_as\_scic\_accuracy} is false throughout.

Calibrated latency claims use only the idle-GPU bakeoff archive (\texttt{bakeoff\_v4}) with \texttt{--calibrate all-gemm}: seven GEMM decisions from few-shot wall-clock of \texttt{always\_fuse} versus \texttt{always\_unfuse}; five attention decisions from library SDPA with a \texttt{compile\_sdpa\_chain} epilogue policy.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
\item SCIC classifies GEMM, attention, norms, and MoE sites by shape-dependent arithmetic intensity against datasheet Roofline ridges before fusion scheduling.
\item A dual-plan selector emits MemMode, ComputeMode, or both in a policy band around the ridge, with optional wall-clock calibration of executable GEMM paths.
\item A library-aware attention epilogue policy times PyTorch SDPA plus RMSNorm under \texttt{torch.compile} even when the analytical primary is ComputeMode.
\item Docker bakeoffs on NVIDIA A40 document selector evolution; idle-GPU calibrated GEMM closes a datasheet-only miss where \texttt{torch.compile} wins on prefill shapes.
\item Evaluation is honest about missing Nsight DRAM bytes and absent custom MemMode/ComputeMode kernels; claims are wall-clock subgraph latency plus logged decisions.
\end{highlights}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Compilers for GPU kernels already fuse a matrix multiply with the elementwise and normalization code that consumes it.
The usual justification is traffic: the product matrix is expensive to write and to read back.
That justification is incomplete.
The same multiply is a different machine regime at decode (a handful of rows) and at prefill (thousands of rows).
A fusion that pays for itself on one side of the ridge can crowd registers and the MMA pipeline on the other.
Attention makes the gap sharper: FlashAttention-style IO and a score-materializing implementation are not two tunings of one intensity; they are different intensities.

This paper argues for \emph{plan-polymorphic fusion} in systems terms:
(1)~intensity is shape-dependent for GEMM, attention, and grouped GEMM;
(2)~a classifier should run before fusion on concrete shape, dtype, and traffic accounting and return a regime rather than a boolean adjacency test;
(3)~the scheduler should emit two plans for one site when needed and pick at runtime from the regime or from a measured cache keyed by shape bucket.

ComFuse~\cite{Mu2025ComFuse} showed that joint MatMul and epilogue fusion is profitable and reported a self-attention case where the joint schedule loses to an IO-aware kernel---a missing switch, not a reason to abandon fusion.
We do not re-derive or implement ComFuse's Stage-Stream execution model.

\textbf{Contributions.}
An analytical SCIC for GEMM, attention (prefill and decode, MHA and GQA), norms, reductions, softmax, elementwise chains, ragged MoE grouped GEMM, and mixed-dtype epilogues, scored against datasheet ridges for H100 FP16/FP8, A100 FP16, and A40 FP16.
A dual-plan selector (MemMode, ComputeMode, or both) with shape-bucketed plan cache and optional few-shot wall-clock calibration (\texttt{--calibrate all-gemm}).
A library-aware attention epilogue policy (\texttt{compile\_sdpa\_chain}) for bakeoffs using PyTorch SDPA.
A correction to byte-fraction intuition for GEMM+RMSNorm fusion under a cold-cache model.
A reproducible evaluation arc---analytical selfcheck, A40 \texttt{torch.matmul} motivation, and Docker bakeoffs archived under \texttt{paper/data/bakeoff\{,\_v2,\_v4\}/}.

\textbf{Non-contributions.}
Stage-Stream; new attention kernels; quoting profiler memory as SCIC accuracy; custom MemMode/ComputeMode CUDA kernels; end-to-end tokens/s.

\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

\subsection{Roofline}
\label{sec:roofline}

Following Williams et al.~\cite{Williams2009Roofline}, arithmetic intensity and the machine ridge are
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{AI} = \frac{\mathrm{FLOPs}}{\mathrm{bytes}}, \qquad
  R = \frac{\mathrm{peak\ FLOP/s}}{\mathrm{peak\ byte/s}}.
\end{equation}
We use this model as a classifier feature, not as a latency predictor.
Table~\ref{tab:ridges} lists datasheet anchors (dense Tensor Core, sparsity off).

  \label{tab:ridges}

    \toprule
    Machine & Peak & Memory & $R$ (FLOP/byte) \\
    \midrule
    H100 SXM, FP16 & 989 TFLOP/s & HBM3 3.35 TB/s & 295.22 \\
    H100 SXM, FP8 & 1979 TFLOP/s & HBM3 3.35 TB/s & 590.75 \\
    A100 80GB SXM, FP16 & 312 TFLOP/s & HBM2e 2.039 TB/s & 153.02 \\
    A40, FP16 & 149.7 TFLOP/s & GDDR6 696 GB/s & 215.09 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

SCIC's three-way label is a policy band around the knee: memory-bound if $\mathrm{AI} < 0.5R$; compute-bound if $\mathrm{AI} > 2R$; ridge-region otherwise.
Inside the band the selector emits both plans because small errors in bytes or in $R$ flip the knee.

\subsection{Fusion modes and traffic}
\label{sec:fusion-modes}

\textbf{MemMode} fuses the epilogue, avoids writing the intermediate, and prefers smaller tiles.
\textbf{ComputeMode} does not pull a heavy epilogue into the MMA mainloop, uses larger tiles, and materializes GEMM outputs when appropriate.
For attention, neither mode materializes the score matrix; modes differ in tile size and whether a further epilogue joins the mainloop.
Bytes are a cold-HBM lower bound: each live tensor moves once.
Neither mode corresponds to a kernel emitted in our artifact (\texttt{kernel\_emitted=false}).

\section{Shape-dependent intensity}
\label{sec:intensity}

\subsection{GEMM}
For $C=AB$ with shapes $[M,K]$, $[K,N]$, $[M,N]$ and element sizes $e_A,e_B,e_C$,
$\mathrm{FLOPs}=2MNK$ and $\mathrm{bytes}=MK e_A + KN e_B + MN e_C$.
For a square projection $N=K=d$ in uniform FP16, $\mathrm{AI}(M)=2Md/((d+2M)e)$.
Setting AI equal to a target $t$ gives $M^\star = Rd/(d-2R)$ for FP16.
On A40 FP16 with $d=4096$, $M^\star \approx 240.3$ (datasheet crossing, not measured).

Decode ($M{=}1$) and prefill ($M{=}2048$) of the same linear layer sit in different regimes; expert token counts in MoE create heterogeneous regimes within one layer (Table~\ref{tab:moe} sketches skew).

\subsection{Attention}
For $Q,K,V$ with shape $[B,H,S,D]$, IO-aware prefill has $\mathrm{AI}=S/e$ (head dimension cancels).
Materialized scores stay memory-bound at every sequence length for typical head widths on H100-class ridges.
Decode AI tends to $2H_q/(H_{kv}e)$ as cache length grows---a prefill ``compute-bound attention'' label is misleading for decode.

\subsection{GEMM+RMSNorm byte fraction}
Fusing RMSNorm saves fraction $2M/(d+4M)$ of unfused bytes in FP16, which \emph{increases} with $M$, while strict Roofline speedup of that fusion stays near $1\times$ when both sides are compute-limited at large $M$.

\section{SCIC}
\label{sec:scic}

A site is a kind, a shape, and per-tensor dtypes.
SCIC returns FLOPs, bytes, AI, AI/$R$, three-way regime, and strict Roofline limit.
The feature is queryable at compile time for concrete shapes and at runtime for bucketed shapes.
Planned calibration with isolated Nsight DRAM bytes remains future work; A40 runs left \texttt{achieved\_ai} empty and \texttt{proxy\_ai} from \texttt{torch.profiler} is explicitly not SCIC accuracy.

\section{Dual-plan selection}
\label{sec:dual-plan}

Given a SCIC report, the prototype applies:
(1)~elementwise chains, reductions, softmax, and norms emit MemMode only;
(2)~memory-bound $\rightarrow$ MemMode; compute-bound $\rightarrow$ ComputeMode;
(3)~ridge-region $\rightarrow$ both plans, primary from AI vs.\ $R$;
(4)~attention never materializes scores; ComputeMode means large tiles and epilogue fission, not score traffic.

For library SDPA bakeoffs, executable attention policy is \texttt{compile\_sdpa\_chain} (\texttt{always\_fuse}) in every regime.
GEMM ridge and compute shapes can take a few-shot wall-clock probe (\texttt{--calibrate all-gemm}): median of \texttt{always\_fuse} vs.\ \texttt{always\_unfuse}; \texttt{selected\_baseline} follows \texttt{measured\_winner} when present.
Shape buckets for the plan cache are $1$, $2$--$8$, $9$--$32$, $33$--$128$, $129$--$512$, $513$--$2048$, $2049$--$8192$, $8193+$.

\section{Position relative to joint fusion}
\label{sec:joint}

Joint MatMul--epilogue fusion asks whether ops can share a kernel; SCIC plus dual plans ask which legal kernel schedule should exist for \emph{this} shape.
ComFuse~\cite{Mu2025ComFuse}, RedFuser~\cite{Tang2026RedFuser}, and Neptune~\cite{Zhao2026Neptune} emit fused kernels; we stop at regime classification and compiler/library path selection.

\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Roofline}~\cite{Williams2009Roofline} supplies the intensity--ridge picture used as a per-site compiler feature.
\textbf{IO-aware attention}~\cite{Dao2022FlashAttention}: we treat FA-style plans as existing schedules SCIC can label; bakeoff SDPA rows are PyTorch library paths, not FlashAttention-3 from this repository.
\textbf{Joint fusion}~\cite{Mu2025ComFuse,Tang2026RedFuser,Zhao2026Neptune}: we cite ComFuse's attention counterexample as motivation for ComputeMode on long sequences without implementing Stage-Stream.
\textbf{Fusion granularity}~\cite{Lacouture2026FuseFlow} studies sparse streaming targets---orthogonal to dense GPU regime labels.
\textbf{Compiler stacks}~\cite{Ansel2024PyTorch2}: \texttt{torch.compile} already fuses many sites; dynamic shapes alone did not flip GEMM epilogue choice when datasheet AI said compute-bound while compile still won---motivating measurement feedback.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

No Nsight and no Docker DRAM AI; \texttt{scic\_selected} copies baseline medians without plan-switch overhead; bakeoffs v1/v2 ran on busy GPUs; cold HBM model omits L2, masks, and paged KV; $0.5R/2R$ band is a knob; datasheet peaks are not measured in-rack peaks; no hand-written MemMode/ComputeMode kernels.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Shape-dependent intensity crosses decode, prefill, attention sequence length, and MoE skew within one model.
SCIC publishes the regime from shape and traffic; the dual plan turns it into MemMode, ComputeMode, or both without owning Stage-Stream.
Device evidence to date is an A40 \texttt{torch.matmul} curve, fusion-proxy timings explicitly not fusion speedups, and Docker bakeoffs that log wall-clock plus decisions.
The idle-GPU \texttt{bakeoff\_v4} archive closes a GEMM selector gap with few-shot wall-clock calibration while attention stays on \texttt{compile\_sdpa\_chain}.
Isolated Nsight DRAM bytes, custom fused kernels, and end-to-end serving throughput remain open.

\section{Formulas implemented in \texttt{shapefuse.intensity}}
\label{app:formulas}

GEMM: $\mathrm{AI}=2MNK/(MK e_A + KN e_B + MN e_C)$.
Square projection: $\mathrm{AI}=2Md/((d+2M)e)$.
FA-style prefill: $\mathrm{AI}=S/e$.
Softmax on materialized scores: $\mathrm{AI}=5/(3e)$.
Regime: memory if $\mathrm{AI}<0.5R$, compute if $\mathrm{AI}>2R$, else ridge.
Roofline seconds: $\max(\mathrm{FLOPs}/P,\mathrm{bytes}/B)$.
